MarsPath measured terrain and imagery update, 2026-10-27

HRSC/MOLA 200 m v2 blended DEM:
https://fdp.astrogeology.usgs.gov/fdp/mars/
https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/Mars/HRSC_MOLA_Blend/Mars_HRSC_MOLA_BlendDEM_Global_200mp_v2.tif
Fergason, R., Hare, T. & Laura, J. (2018), HRSC and MOLA Blended
Digital Elevation Model at 200m v2, USGS Astrogeology.
Original: 106694 x 53347 signed Int16, nodata -32768, uncompressed
little-endian BigTIFF. Geographic Mars 2000 sphere, radius 3396190 m.
Upper-left corner -180,90; spacing 0.003374120830641 degrees.
Four small OFFLINE crops are bundled: Jezero context, Gale, Valles,
Olympus. Their exact pixel bounds are in RegionalDem200.json.
These crops do not provide global 200 m coverage in this application.
Source file is read with verified HTTP byte ranges; no resampling of
stored crop heights. Runtime bilinear sampling uses pixel centres.
Detailed Jezero source: JezeroFullSource.txt, 20 m CTX DEM.
Fallback elsewhere: bundled 16 ppd global MGS MOLA (~3.7 km equator).
The 200 m grid is a blend; its pixel spacing is not a uniform claim
of independently measured 200 m landforms throughout every crop.

Regional planetary imagery:
https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=1efb16809db84f0c892b9b0662dab0c8
https://astro.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/OnMars/MDIM/MapServer
NASA/JPL/USGS, NASA Ames colorized Viking MDIM 2.1, hosted by Esri.
Published service native source: 231 m at equator; positional accuracy
approximately one source pixel. Stored display is an 8192 x 4096
assembly of level 3, 512-pixel source tiles: ~2.6 km display sampling
at equator. No invented detail or AI upscaling. Source JPEG tiles are
spatially assembled; display JPEG is encoded at quality 94.
This colorized mosaic is processed orbital imagery, not a calibrated
natural-color measurement at every visible point.

Ground color/lighting references:
https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/detailed-panorama-of-mars-jezero-crater-delta/
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/explore-mars-jezero-crater-with-nasas-perseverance-rover-2/
NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS. Small soil grain and boulders are modeled;
the imagery is not used to invent surveyed rock positions or hazards.

Ground scene: 1 Unity unit = 1 metre, source macro heights 1:1.
Regional close maps: 3.5x CTX / 2x other source relief, labelled model.
Long-route maps use spherical Mars geometry and source elevation,
with 2x source relief to aid inspection. Finer local geometry replaces
the corresponding coarse planetary mesh, without a floating map edge.
