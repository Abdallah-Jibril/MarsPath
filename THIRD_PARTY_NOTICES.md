# Third-Party Notices and Data Attribution

## Planetary data and photographs

Product-level notices identify NASA mission imagery, USGS Astrogeology, HiRISE, MOLA, CTX, and HRSC/HRSC-MOLA sources. Follow the producers' use terms and preserve their credits when redistributing assets or figures.

See [Data Sources](docs/DATA_SOURCES.md), GIS README files, and adjacent resource manifests. Display tinting, modeled rocks, generated hazard icons, and illustrative celestial backgrounds are separate from surveyed measurements.

This independent prototype uses producer names for source attribution and challenge context. They do not imply endorsement or certification.
